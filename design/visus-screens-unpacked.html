25About & disclaimer
About
VisusVersion 1.0

Quick eye screening you can do at home, in a few minutes.

It does
Screen your vision
Track changes over time
Remind you to rest your eyes
It doesn't
Diagnose conditions
Replace an eye exam
Prescribe glasses or medicine
Your data stays on your phoneTests run on the device. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a report. The camera only measures distance and never records.
Screening, not diagnosisResults point out signs worth checking. Only an eye doctor can diagnose. If your vision changes suddenly, see one right away.
